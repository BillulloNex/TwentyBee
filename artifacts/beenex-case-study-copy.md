## 1. Yunique Medical

Current page: https://beenex.ai/work/yunique-ghl-charm

### Page copy

**Eyebrow**  
Delivered healthcare integration · 2026

**Headline**  
Patient intake and appointments, connected to the clinical record

**Introduction**  
Yunique Medical used GoHighLevel for patient contact and intake workflows and Charm Health for the clinical record. BeeNex connected the two so patient creation, appointment changes, and completed intake could move through a defined workflow instead of relying on repeated entry.

**At a glance**  
Client: Yunique Medical  
Systems: GoHighLevel and Charm Health  
Scope: Four integration areas  
Status: Reported live in production on August 31, 2026

**The challenge**  
Information collected before a visit needed to reach the clinical system. Contacts, bookings, reschedules, cancellations, and intake forms crossed a boundary between GoHighLevel and Charm. Without that connection, staff had to carry information from one workflow into another.

**What BeeNex delivered**

- **Patient creation:** A GoHighLevel contact can become a patient record in Charm.
- **Appointment updates:** Booking, rescheduling, and cancellation are included in the integration.
- **Intake transfer:** Completed intake information moves into the corresponding clinical encounter.
- **Patient-specific data:** The delivered scope also includes custom compounds for individual patients.

BeeNex also supplied an operations guide with steps for checking the integration and supporting it after launch.

**The outcome**  
The project was reported complete and live in production on August 31, 2026. It connected four workflow areas—patient creation, appointments, intake forms, and custom compounds—between GoHighLevel and Charm Health.

**Who this example is useful for**  
This is an example of connecting an intake or CRM system to an EHR. It is most relevant when a practice is trying to reduce the handoff between patient contact, booking, intake, and the clinical record.

## 2. Mental Health America of the Palm Beaches

Current page: https://beenex.ai/work/mental-health-america

### Page copy

**Eyebrow**  
Delivered and used · Community behavioral health · 2025

**Headline**  
One place for case notes across three community programs

**Introduction**  
Staff at Mental Health America of the Palm Beaches documented their work on paper, by email, and in spreadsheets. BeeNex built one case-note application for three programs. The published project results report that a case note now takes 5–8 minutes to complete, down from 15–20 minutes.

**At a glance**  
Client: Mental Health America of the Palm Beaches  
Programs: Three  
Standardized forms: Eight  
Reported result: 60% less time to complete a case note

**The challenge**  
The Clubhouses, Mental Health Court, and Peer Place programs each had their own paperwork and note-taking routines. Staff recorded information in different places and formats. Supervisors had no single view of case notes, and finding records for review took hours of manual searching.

**What BeeNex delivered**  
BeeNex replaced those scattered processes with an application staff can use on a phone, tablet, or desktop. It gives the three programs eight standardized forms and a shared way to find the records they need. Staff can dictate notes, while supervisors can search, review, and export records. Role-based access and activity logs help the organization control and review how the system is used. Submission retries help staff working with unreliable connections.

**The result**  
The published project results report that case-note completion fell from 15–20 minutes to 5–8 minutes. Three programs now use one platform and eight standardized forms. Supervisors can filter and export records instead of searching through paper, email, and spreadsheets.
